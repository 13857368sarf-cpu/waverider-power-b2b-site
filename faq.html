Technical Questions

What is the difference between 2-stroke and 4-stroke outboards?

Two-stroke models emphasize simple construction and power-to-weight, while four-stroke models generally offer quieter and more fuel-efficient operation.

How do I choose the correct shaft length?

Shaft length should match the boat transom height and installation requirements.

Do you offer manual and electric start?

Selected models can support manual or electric start configurations.

Are tiller and remote control options available?

Availability depends on model and horsepower. Confirm the required control type in your inquiry.

How should horsepower be selected?

Consider boat size, hull type, load, operating conditions and local regulations.

Do engines receive water tank testing?

Testing procedures include operating checks appropriate to the production and inspection process.
