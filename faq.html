Buyer Questions

Outboard Motor Supplier FAQ

Answers for dealers, distributors, boat builders, fleet buyers and repair shops.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on horsepower, configuration and whether the order mixes models. Send your target models and quantity for a practical dealer proposal.

Can I mix different horsepower models in one order?

Mixed-model orders can be discussed for qualified B2B buyers, subject to model availability and packing requirements.

Do you support OEM outboard motor orders?

Yes. OEM support can include logo, labels, color, cartons, manuals and dealer package requirements.

Can WaveRider Power produce a private label product line?

Yes. Private label support is available for qualified bulk orders after confirming branding, specifications and packaging.

What warranty do you provide?

WaveRider Power provides a standard 12-month warranty with dealer communication and spare parts support.

Do you supply outboard motor spare parts?

Yes. We support propellers, water pumps, fuel pumps, carburetors, gearbox parts, ignition coils, control cables and maintenance kits.

What is the typical production lead time?

Lead time varies by model, quantity, configuration and branding. It is confirmed in the formal quotation.

How do you ship outboard motors internationally?

Shipment can be arranged by sea or other agreed logistics methods depending on quantity and destination.

Do you offer export wooden crate packing?

Yes. Export wooden crate packing is available to protect outboard motors during international transportation.

How can I receive dealer pricing?

Submit your company, country, buyer type, models and estimated quantity through the Quote page.

What payment terms are available?

Payment terms are confirmed according to order value, customization and production schedule in the sales agreement.

What is the difference between 2-stroke and 4-stroke outboards?

Two-stroke models are often valued for simple construction and power-to-weight, while four-stroke models generally offer quieter operation and fuel efficiency.

Which 2-stroke models do you supply?

Available 2-stroke horsepower ranges should be confirmed against your target market and current production schedule.

Which 4-stroke models do you supply?

WaveRider Power supplies selected 4-stroke models within the 4HP–60HP range.

Can you support boat builders?

Yes. We help boat builders with horsepower selection, shaft length, control configuration and private label requirements.

Can you supply fleet buyers?

Yes. Fleet buyers can request commercial-use model recommendations and maintenance parts planning.

Do you work with repair shops?

Yes. Repair shops can request spare parts lists and model-specific parts support.

Can you recommend the right horsepower for my boat market?

Yes. Share boat type, size, load, operating conditions and target market for a recommendation.

Can you customize cartons and manuals?

Yes. Carton and manual customization can be discussed as part of an OEM or private label order.

How do I request a catalog and spare parts list?

Use the Quote page and select the relevant product interest, or contact WaveRider Power directly on WhatsApp.
