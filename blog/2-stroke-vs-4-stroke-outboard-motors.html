2-Stroke vs 4-Stroke Outboard Motors

Compare 2-stroke and 4-stroke outboard motors for B2B sourcing.

2-stroke outboards are often selected for lower initial cost, lighter structure and simpler service in price-sensitive markets. 4-stroke outboards are often selected where fuel economy, lower noise and cleaner operation are important. Dealers should choose based on local regulation, customer budget, service capability and spare parts plan.
