40HP Outboard Motor FAQ

What boat types commonly use a 40HP outboard?

40HP outboards are commonly used on fishing boats, rental boats, patrol boats and utility boats where buyers need stronger daily-use power than portable small models.

Can 40HP models be supplied for OEM boat packages?

Yes. Boat builders and OEM package buyers can discuss model configuration, branding, carton, manual and spare parts requirements.

What information is needed for a 40HP quote?

Please send quantity, country, boat type, shaft length, start method, steering/control preference and whether spare parts or OEM packaging are required.
