15HP Outboard Motor FAQ

Is 15HP enough for a small fishing boat?

15HP is commonly used for small fishing boats, tenders and light utility boats. Final selection depends on boat size, hull type, load and operating water conditions.

Should dealers stock 2-stroke or 4-stroke 15HP models?

2-stroke models are often attractive for price-sensitive and simpler-service markets. 4-stroke models fit buyers who care more about fuel efficiency, lower noise and emissions expectations.

Can I order 15HP outboards with my own brand?

OEM and private label options can be discussed for qualified bulk orders, including logo, carton, manual and dealer package requirements.
