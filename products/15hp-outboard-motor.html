15HP FAQ

Questions from Dealers and Boat Buyers

What engine type is the 15HP outboard motor?

It is a 2-cylinder, 2-stroke outboard motor with 246cc displacement and carburetor fuel delivery.

What is the rated output power?

The rated output is 11kW at 5000rpm.

Is short shaft and long shaft available?

Yes. The short shaft is 440mm and the long shaft is 567mm. Confirm your boat transom height before ordering.

What is the engine weight?

The weight range is approximately 36–38kg depending on shaft configuration.

How does the engine start?

This configuration uses a manual starting system with tiller steering.

What boats are suitable for this 15HP model?

Typical applications include fishing boats, inflatable boats, leisure boats and light commercial boats. Final selection depends on hull, load and operating conditions.

Can I order OEM or private label packaging?

Yes. Qualified B2B orders can discuss logo printing, color, carton and manual customization.

Do you supply spare parts for dealers?

Yes. Dealer orders can include propellers, water pump parts, fuel system parts, gearbox parts, ignition parts and maintenance kits.
