Buyer Questions

How do I choose between 2-stroke and 4-stroke?

2-stroke models are often selected for simpler maintenance and price-sensitive markets. 4-stroke models are preferred where fuel efficiency, lower noise and emissions expectations matter more.

Can you recommend configuration for my boat?

Yes. Send boat length, load, use case, water type and target speed through the quote form. Our team can recommend horsepower and configuration.

Do you supply spare parts with bulk orders?

Yes. Dealer orders can include recommended spare parts kits to support local after-sales service.
