60HP Outboard Motor FAQ

Who should choose a 60HP outboard motor?

60HP is usually considered by commercial boat users, fishing fleets, patrol boats and buyers who need stronger propulsion than 25HP or 40HP ranges.

Is spare parts support important for 60HP buyers?

Yes. Commercial 60HP users often run higher operating hours, so dealers and fleets should plan spare parts together with the engine order.

Can Kanglongsi Marine support distributors for 60HP models?

Yes. Distributors can request dealer pricing, configuration support, OEM/private label discussion, export packing and recommended spare parts packages.
