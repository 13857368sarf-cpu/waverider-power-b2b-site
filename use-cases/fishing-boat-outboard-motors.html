Fishing Boat Outboard FAQ

Is 15HP enough for a fishing boat?

15HP can fit small fishing boats and light-load use. Larger boats, heavier loads or commercial operating hours may require 40HP or 60HP ranges.

Should fishing operators choose 2-stroke or 4-stroke?

2-stroke models can fit price-sensitive and simple-service markets. 4-stroke models are often preferred for fuel efficiency, lower noise and emissions expectations.

Can you include spare parts for fishing boat operators?

Yes. Recommended spare parts kits can be included with dealer or fleet orders to support local repair needs.
