Outboard Motor FAQ

Do you supply both 2-stroke and 4-stroke outboard motors?

Yes. WaveRider Power offers 2-stroke and 4-stroke outboard motors across selected horsepower ranges, with model recommendations based on buyer market, boat type and emissions requirements.

Can you support OEM or private label orders?

Yes. We can discuss logo, color, carton, manual and dealer package requirements for qualified bulk orders.

What warranty do you provide?

Standard support is 12 months, with spare parts and technical guidance for dealers and distributors.

Who are your typical B2B customers?

Dealers, distributors, boat builders, repair shops, fleet buyers, fishing operators and bulk procurement teams.
